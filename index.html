Client reviews

Loved by conveyancers who made the switch.

Hear from the practices already running their matters on Realtime Conveyancer.

Full review from Carmela Criceli & Brad Eckermann

“Changing to Realtime Conveyancer has been the biggest but also the best change to the business in 15 years! Coming from a Practice Management Software system that was outdated, having very few integrations with third systems and with a substantial amount of manual processes and workarounds to suit e-conveyancing, we knew that any new system we implemented had to be extremely valuable and efficient for our team- Realtime Conveyancer is definitely that and more. Not only does Realtime Conveyancer have extremely cool features and integrations which vary from email and SMS capabilities, internal document and workflow creations, etc. the system even has an App that is accessible by clients and Real Estate Agents to share documents through as well as to communicate with and keep the clients fully up to date with where their settlement is at. Further to this and one of the most exciting features of the platform relates to the financials, especially the automation of reconciling the finances for settlement as well as receipting and journaling post settlement. The process is so simple from a practical perspective, yet so effective and efficient while providing our clients with the best experience. Realtime Conveyancer is truly a gamechanger within our industry!”

Carmela Criceli & Brad Eckermann
